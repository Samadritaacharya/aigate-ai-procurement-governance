# AIGate product specification

## Product promise

**From AI request to approved, governed and measurable production use.**

AIGate is a portfolio-safe AI procurement and governance control plane for organizations that need to decide whether an AI use case should proceed, what evidence is missing, who must approve it, and whether the value case is credible.

## Primary users

- AI / digital-transformation leads
- procurement and vendor-management teams
- enterprise architecture and security
- privacy / DPO teams
- legal / AI-governance functions
- product and business owners
- PMO / portfolio governance

## Jobs to be done

1. Intake a proposed AI use case in a structured way.
2. Screen it for prohibited/high-risk/transparency indicators without pretending to make a legal determination.
3. Identify privacy, security, vendor, evaluation, human-oversight and monitoring evidence gaps.
4. Route the use case through an explainable approval path.
5. Quantify benefit, run cost, first-year ROI and payback.
6. Preserve an evidence graph connecting owner, vendor, model, controls, approval and value.
7. Keep optional model reasoning advisory; deterministic policy owns release authority.

## Non-goals

- legal advice or conformity assessment
- automated authorization of real production AI systems
- automated procurement commitments
- storage of real confidential vendor/customer data in the public demo

## Product modes

### Community / portfolio mode
Zero-key Next.js + Python/FastAPI, synthetic data, deterministic policy, local browser history, optional local model endpoint.

### Future commercial mode
Multi-tenant Postgres, RBAC/SSO, AI inventory, vendor evidence ingestion, connectors, exportable control packs, workflow integrations, EU-region hosting and paid support/SLA.
